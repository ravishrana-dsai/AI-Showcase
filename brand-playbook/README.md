# Sports Tech Brand Playbook

> A complete, AI-assisted brand strategy for a player intelligence platform in racquet sports. Covers mission, positioning, voice and tone, messaging architecture, visual identity, content strategy, and brand-in-action guidelines across 7 chapters.

## What This Is

A working brand bible built to serve as the single source of truth for how an AI-powered sports analytics platform looks, sounds, and behaves across every touchpoint. Designed to be used by product, marketing, design, and leadership simultaneously.

Each chapter is a standalone document that cross-references the others.

## AI Involvement

This playbook was developed with AI-assisted strategy work using Claude:
- Positioning frameworks and competitive differentiation analysis
- Voice and tone architecture with vocabulary guidelines
- Messaging hierarchy and channel-specific adaptation
- Content strategy with format and cadence recommendations
