# Meridian — Investor Deck Brief

## Company
Meridian is a B2B SaaS platform that helps mid-market logistics companies unify fragmented supply chain data into a single real-time operations hub. Founded in 2022, headquartered in Austin, TX.

## Audience
Series A investors. Assume familiarity with SaaS metrics but not deep logistics domain knowledge.

## Deck Goal
Convince investors that (1) the market is large and underserved, (2) Meridian's product is meaningfully differentiated, and (3) the go-to-market motion is proven and scalable.

## Slide Structure (6 slides)
1. Title slide — "Unifying the Fragmented Supply Chain"
2. Market Opportunity — $42B TAM in supply chain visibility software; mid-market segment ($100M–$1B revenue companies) is underserved by legacy ERPs and point solutions
3. The Problem — logistics teams juggle 7+ disconnected tools on average; lack of real-time data costs them 12–18% in operational inefficiency
4. Product Overview — Meridian's unified data layer: integrates with 200+ carrier/WMS/ERP APIs, real-time dashboard, AI-powered anomaly detection, mobile-first alerts
5. Go-to-Market — land-and-expand motion; initial land via ops teams ($25K ACV), expand to enterprise ($120K ACV); current: 48 customers, $2.1M ARR, 140% NRR
6. Key Takeaways

## Tone
Confident, data-driven, concise. No fluff. Every slide should have a single clear point.

## Brand Notes
Dark navy background, teal accents. Professional but modern.
